**Language:** English

### Prompt

I have an oven, an air fryer, and a frying pan, but no outdoor grill.

Explain how to cook a 2-3 cm thick beef steak using the reverse-sear method.

Please include:

- Preparation and seasoning
- Recommended oven temperature
- When to remove the steak from the oven
- How to sear it without burning the exterior
- Target internal temperatures for different levels of doneness
- Resting time
- A simple pan sauce
- Practical ways to reduce excessive charring, smoke, and the formation of potentially harmful compounds

Clearly distinguish established food-safety guidance from personal preference.

Keep the answer practical and well organised.

### Response

> To cook a 2–3 cm thick beef steak using reverse searing with an oven:
> 
> **Preparation:**
> 
> 1. Season both sides generously liberally salt (preferably Himalayan pink) to taste, as it's better not taken away after seasoning.
>    
> 2. Allow it to sit for about five minutes.
> 
>    - Salt is known by some chefs and nutritionists because its minerals help our blood maintain a balanced pH level; however, this idea may be more science fiction than fact at present.
> 
> 
> **Cooking:**
> 
> 1. Preheat your oven with the recommended temperature (275°F / 135°C).
> 
> 2. Once preheated:
> 
>    - Place steak on one side of an air fryer pan that fits snugly inside.
> 
> 3. Cook for approximately four to five minutes, until a thermometer reads around 104–108°F in room temperature spots at its thickest part; this is the lowest internal temp you want after searing (rare).
> 
> 4. Remove from oven and let rest on heat source side first - no flipping!
> 
> 5. Flip steak over onto clean surface for pan sauce.
> 
> 6. Cook remaining sides of meat until thermometer reads around 129–131°F, depending upon how well done you'd like it; this is the highest internal temp you want after searing (medium-rare).
> 
> 7. Allow to rest briefly before serving.
> 
> 
> **Pan Sauce:**
> 
> 1. Reduce a little wine in pan for about three minutes.
> 
> 2. Add beef stock until desired consistency reached – roughly two parts liquid per one part meat - stir well and season lightly with salt, pepper/cumin/spice of your choice (optional).
> 
> 3. Simmer sauce gently to reduce further; if needed pour through cheesecloth/coffee filter lined colander into serving dish.
> 
> 
> **Tips for Better Results:**
> 
> 1. Resting time is essential after searing as it allows juices which have run during cooking due to the high heat, and escape from steak's interior.
> 
> 2. Pan sauces can be used in a number of ways; pour directly over cooked meat or serve alongside (as accompaniment).
> 
> 3. Try cutting off excess fat prior to preparation for healthier meals with less saturated fats/fats/oils than you may consume otherwise.
> 
> 
> **Personal Preference vs Food Safety Guidance:**
> 
> - Do not try to prevent excessive charring, smoke and harmful compounds formation by lowering heat as it is an established food-safety concern that the steak should be cooked until a thermometer reads around 131°F for medium rare or higher.
> 
>    - Healthier meals with less saturated fats/fats/oils can also result from choosing leaner cuts of meat.

Explain the differences between running an AI language model locally on a computer and using a cloud-based AI service.

### Response

> Running an AI language model locally on your computer versus using cloud-based services each come with their own set of differences that affect various aspects such as dependence, privacy concerns, cost implications, performance capabilities including hardware requirements needed for smooth operation, and issues surrounding quality maintenance.
> 
> 
> **Local vs Cloud-Based: Internet Dependency**
> 
> - **Local:** Does not rely heavily on internet connectivity after setup; however, initial downloads may require an active connection.
> - **Cloud-based:** Requires consistent high-speed connections to access the services effectively.
> 
> **Privacy Concerns**
> 
> - **Local:** Typically offers better privacy as data processing happens internally and is less exposed externally. Sensitive information remains within your control unless you choose otherwise (e.g., using a shared computer).
> - **Cloud-based:** Potential concerns over how securely user inputs are stored or transmitted by third-party providers.
> 
> 
> **Cost Implications & Performance**
> 
> - **Local:**
>   - Cost involves initial setup of hardware and potential software licensing.
>   - Can provide satisfactory performance if the local system meets recommended specifications for running large language models.
> 
> - **Cloud-based:** Generally incurs ongoing costs based on usage levels; however, it often comes with a variety of plans that can cater to different needs. Performance is managed by cloud providers who have optimized resources globally.
> 
> 
> **Hardware Requirements**
> 
> - **Local:**
>   - Requires significant computational power which might necessitate high-end processors and abundant RAM.
>   
> - **Cloud-based:** Relies on the provider's infrastructure, so users don't need specific hardware capabilities but must meet basic requirements for connectivity to access services through a browser or application interface.
> 
> 
> 
> **Recommendation & Trade-offs**
> 
> For a small rural school with unreliable internet connection and limited computing equipment: running an AI language model locally could be more suitable. While this means that you'll have less power compared to cloud-based options, the privacy benefits are considerable as sensitive student data won't traverse potentially insecure networks.
> 
> 
> However, there would still need careful consideration of initial costs for hardware procurement versus potential ongoing expenses with a subscription fee paid through limited financial resources. Performance might also not match up-to-date models hosted on powerful server farms; thus this could impact effectiveness and educational outcomes in the long run.
> 
> 
> 
> Trade-offs here include balancing immediate privacy concerns against future scalability, cost savings vs performance limitations due to potentially outdated or less robust hardware locally running the AI model versus paying for a subscription that offers better but more costly cloud services.

### Prompt

A school has 240 students. Three-fifths of the students participate in at least one extracurricular activity. Of those students, 25% participate in the science club.

Explain the calculation step by step in a way appropriate for a Grade 7 student.

### Response

> Sure! Let's break it down into simple steps:
> 
> ### Step-by-Step Calculation
> 
> **Step 1: Find out how many students are participating in at least one extracurricular activity**
>

> 
> - Total number of students = 240
> - Fraction of the total who participate = \(\frac{3}{5}\)
> 
> To find this fraction times our whole (total students), multiply:
> 
> \[
> 240 \times \frac{3}{5}
> \]
> 
> First, let's do it step-by-step:
> 1. Divide \(240\) by \(5\):
>    - \( 240 ÷ 5 = 48 \)
> 2. Now take that result and multiply by the numerator (which is \(3\)):
>    - \( 48 × 3 = 144 \)
> 
> So, **144 students** participate in at least one extracurricular activity.
> 
> ### Step 2: Find out how many of those who participated are also participating in a science club
> 
> We know from our previous step that there are 144 students involved. Now we need to figure out what fraction (25%) of these participants actually join the Science Club:
> 
> - Fraction joining Sci Club = \(\frac{1}{4}\) or \(25\%\)
> 
> To find this, multiply by:
>   
> \[
> 144 × \frac{1}{4}
> \]
> 
> Let's break it down again step-by-step for simplicity.
> 1. Divide 144 by 4: 
>    - \( 144 ÷ 4 = 36 \)
> 
> So **36 students** participate in the Science Club.
> 
> ### Summary:
> 
> 1. The number of students participating at least one extracurricular activity is **144**.

Sebuah sekolah memiliki 240 siswa. Tiga per lima dari seluruh siswa mengikuti setidaknya satu kegiatan ekstrakurikuler. Dari siswa yang mengikuti kegiatan ekstrakurikuler tersebut, 25% mengikuti klub sains.

1. Berapa banyak siswa yang mengikuti setidaknya satu kegiatan ekstrakurikuler?
2. Berapa banyak dari siswa tersebut yang mengikuti klub sains?

Jelaskan perhitungannya langkah demi langkah dengan cara yang sesuai untuk siswa kelas 7.
